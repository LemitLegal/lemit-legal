Lemit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Lemit app ("Lemit", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Lemit iOS app (bundle identifier com.lemit.ndiyz).

Lemit is a propagation journal. You log plant cuttings, record checks with photos and short voice notes, and see your own rooting and success rates.

1. Information you create in Lemit

When you use Lemit, the app stores the following on your iPhone so your journal works:

Information Examples Why
Journal entries Plant name, propagation medium, node and leaf count, cutting date, source, notes, stage checks, results To show your cuttings, timeline and success rates
Photos Photos of cuttings you take with the camera or pick from your library To show the progress of each cutting
Voice notes Short recordings (up to 30 seconds) attached to a cutting or check To keep a spoken note with the entry
Custom plants Plants you add to the plant library To make them selectable when you log a cutting
Preferences Default medium, check interval, daily check time, measurement units To mark cuttings as due and to prefill forms
App state A randomly generated identifier created when you tap Log My First Cutting, session state, drafts, hidden sample cuttings To keep your journal and session working
Sprigs Your Sprig balance, the welcome gift flag, and items unlocked with Sprigs To provide purchased and gifted content

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and no feature of Lemit requires personal details.

This information is kept in Lemit's app storage on your device. Lemit does not upload your journal entries, photos or voice notes to us, and we do not have access to them.

2. Permissions

Lemit asks for permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can change any of them later in iOS Settings.

Permission When it is requested What it is used for
Camera When you open the photo guide to photograph a cutting Taking photos of your cutting and its roots
Photo Library (read) When you choose a photo from your library Picking an existing photo of a cutting
Photo Library (add) When you save a comparison card to Photos Saving the exported card to your library
Microphone When you record a voice note Recording a short note about a cutting
Notifications The first time you reach the Growing screen Lemit sends notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

If you decline a permission, the rest of Lemit keeps working. For example, you can still pick photos from your library if the camera is off, and you can still save a cutting without a voice note.

3. Photos and voice notes

  • Photos and voice notes you add are saved inside Lemit's app storage and shown only in the entries you attach them to.
  • Lemit does not apply filters, does not analyze the content of your photos, and does not transcribe your voice notes.
  • When you export a comparison card or PDF report, the file is created on your device. It is saved to Photos or shared only when you choose to do so through the iOS share sheet; the destination you pick (for example Messages or Mail) handles the file under its own privacy terms.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Lemit does not collect facial data. It does not use Face ID, TrueDepth data, face detection, face recognition or any facial geometry. Lemit is designed for photos of plant cuttings. A photo you take or choose may incidentally show a person (for example a hand or face in the background); Lemit treats such a photo like any other photo and does not identify or measure anyone in it.

4.2 How we use it

Photos are used only to display the cutting entry they belong to and to build exports (comparison card, PDF report) that you request. No facial information is derived from them for any purpose.

4.3 Sharing and disclosure

Lemit does not share, sell or disclose photos or any facial information to us or to third parties. Photos are stored in Lemit's app storage on your device. A photo leaves the app only when you export a card or report and choose where to send it.

4.4 Retention

Photos are kept until you delete the photo, delete the cutting, use Delete All Data, or delete the app.

4.5 Scope of this section

This Section 4 is our complete explanation of the collection, use, disclosure, sharing and retention of any facial data in Lemit.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases of Sprigs are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's privacy policy.
  • No AI services. Lemit does not send your entries, photos or voice notes to any artificial intelligence or analysis service.
  • No advertising or analytics. Lemit contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Legal pages. When you open this policy or the Terms of Use inside Lemit, the page is loaded from our website (GitHub Pages), which receives standard technical request data such as your IP address, as with any web page.

6. In-App Purchases and Sprigs

Lemit offers Sprigs, an in-app currency, as consumable purchases through Apple. Sprigs can be spent in the app on clean comparison cards, PDF reports, an unlimited plant library and the Export Pass. New users receive a one-time welcome gift of Sprigs. Your balance and unlocks are recorded in Lemit's app storage on your device so the app can apply them.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a photo, voice note or cutting from its screen at any time.
  • End Session (Profile) closes your current session and returns you to the welcome screen. Your journal stays on the device.
  • Delete All Data (Profile) permanently erases everything you created in Lemit on this device: cuttings, checks, photos, voice notes, custom plants, drafts, preferences, your Sprig balance and unlocks, and app state. Built-in sample cuttings and the built-in plant library are part of the app and appear again afterwards. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes Lemit's app storage.

8. Children's privacy

Lemit is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Lemit does not ask anyone for personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data security

Your Lemit data is protected by the security features of iOS, including app sandboxing and device encryption when your iPhone is locked with a passcode.

10. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
