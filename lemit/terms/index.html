1. The service

Lemit is a propagation journal for plant cuttings. With Lemit you can:

  • Log cuttings with plant, propagation medium, node and leaf count, date, source, photos, notes and short voice notes, one at a time or in a batch.
  • Record checks through four stages (Cutting, Callus, Rooting, Ready) or mark a cutting as failed with a reason.
  • See your cuttings on the Growing screen, in the timeline and in the journal, with due and overdue markers based on your check interval.
  • See success rates and rooting times calculated from your log, by plant, medium, month and failure reason.
  • Browse a built-in plant library and add your own plants.
  • Export comparison cards and PDF reports.
  • Explore built-in sample cuttings, which you can hide at any time.

Lemit is a record-keeping tool. Plant names, typical rooting times and stage descriptions are general guidance only and are not horticultural, agricultural or professional advice. Results depend on your plants and conditions.

2. Getting started and sessions

  • Tap Log My First Cutting on the welcome screen to start. Lemit creates a random identifier for your journal; no personal details are required.

  • Delete All Data (Profile) permanently erases everything you created in Lemit on the device, including your Sprig balance and items unlocked with Sprigs. This cannot be undone.
